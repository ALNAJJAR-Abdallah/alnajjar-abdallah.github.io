Internship · 2026 · 4 weeks

AI Expert Hub, an AI agent portal

A portal that makes a catalogue of AI agents simple to use: find the expert, give it context, resume the conversation.

Context

Capgemini Maroc, around an internal platform that gives access to a catalogue of agents and language models. Their value depends on how easily a user finds the right expert, gives it context and resumes their work.

Initial problem

How can a catalogue of AI agents be made usable by a business user, while keeping security, conversation context and the ability to adapt the experts?

My personal contribution

  • I designed the portal experience and built the frontend (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and the backend (Python, FastAPI).
  • I structured local persistence (SQLite) and built the integration with the company’s agent platform.
  • I evolved the product from functional testing: multiple conversations, files, agent management.

Features delivered

Catalogue
Dynamic agents with search, filters, favourites and pagination.
Expert recommendation
The need is described in natural language; the portal compares the terms with names, roles, descriptions and tags, and ranks the most relevant profiles.
Independent conversations
Each conversation keeps its own context: history, rename, resume and delete.
Text, files and voice
Attachments and voice dictation using the browser’s speech recognition.
Local experts
Create experts, or a customised local copy of an existing agent, without changing the original catalogue. SQLite storage, photo automatically cropped.
Agent platform integration
The catalogue and models are retrieved through an API and exchanges happen in real time. The access key stays on the server; external errors become understandable messages.

What I took from it

  • Web development, REST APIs, WebSocket, data validation and SQLite persistence.
  • Session management, file handling and integrating an AI service into a complete application.
  • Turning a general need into testable features, separating responsibilities and diagnosing each step of an external integration.
