Career Strategy

My career project.

The roles I’m aiming for, the environment I’m looking for and how I’m building my path.

Goals

Short term
A final-year internship in AI from February 2027, preferably at a large company, in Morocco or abroad.
Then
An AI engineer role, at the crossroads of data, modelling and application development.

Target roles

  1. 01

    AI / machine learning engineer

    Developing and evaluating models, NLP and computer vision.

  2. 02

    AI application development

    Integrating language models, AI agents and external services.

  3. 03

    Data and automation

    Data preparation and business-process automation.

Sectors

  • Consulting and digital services
  • Software vendors
  • Banking
  • Insurance
  • Industry

Market context

The World Economic Forum’s Future of Jobs Report 2025 ranks AI and machine learning specialists among the fastest-growing jobs between 2025 and 2030, and AI and Big Data among the skills expected to see the largest rise in demand. In Morocco, the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy targets 240,000 direct jobs in the digital sector by 2030.

These are global projections and a national target for the whole digital sector, not a count of AI job openings available in Morocco today.

Preferred environment

A setting where I can learn from experienced professionals, contribute to useful projects and gradually gain autonomy. I assess each assignment by its content, supervision, technical resources, pay and room to grow.

Action plan

  1. 01

    A consistent profile

    One message across my CV, LinkedIn and this portfolio.

  2. 02

    Showcased achievements

    Present each internship and project through its usefulness, my contribution and its results.

  3. 03

    Deeper AI expertise

    Keep building skills in applied AI: language models, agents and data.

  4. 04

    Targeted opportunities

    Identify companies and assignments aligned with my goals, and tailor each application to the offer.

Sources
